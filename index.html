What moves EUR/USD and CHF/USD?
5 key drivers · tap to expand
▼
Currency rates shift constantly. Before you set your prediction, here are the five forces that tend to move the Euro and Franc against the Dollar — knowing them gives you an edge.
01
Interest rates
The Fed, ECB, and SNB set rates. Higher rates attract foreign capital seeking better returns — lifting that currency. A hawkish Fed surprises often spike the Dollar.
02
Inflation data
CPI prints above expectations? Central banks may hike rates — bullish for that currency. Below-target inflation signals easing ahead — usually bearish.
03
Economic growth
Strong GDP, PMI, or jobs data signals a healthy economy. Markets reward strong economies with stronger currencies. Weak US NFP often weakens the Dollar fast.
04
Risk sentiment
When markets panic, investors flee to safe havens — the Swiss Franc and Dollar both benefit. Risk-on rallies often weaken CHF and USD as capital chases higher returns elsewhere.
05
Politics & headlines
Elections, trade tensions, central bank speeches, and geopolitical shocks move rates sharply and quickly. This week's news context above is your guide.
💬 Tip: Check the market news above before predicting. A single central bank speech can move EUR/USD by +0.5% in minutes.

The game
01
Predict
Every Monday, submit your price targets for EUR/USD and CHF/USD by Wednesday midnight. Just a number — where do you think the pair closes on Friday?
02
Score
Friday close prices determine your score. Accuracy is measured by how close your % move call is to the actual move. The closer your call, the higher your score. Consistent accuracy builds your rank.
03
Win
Weekly winner gets a bottle of Veuve Clicquot. Season champion wins a Tissot PRX Powermatic 80 watch. Everyone is ranked publicly — your reputation is on the line.
Scoring formula
Score per pair = 100 − (|your % call − actual % move| × 100)
i.e. each 0.01% off costs 1 point · max 100 pts per pair per week · weekly total = 200 pts max
Season score = sum of all weekly scores (max 1600 pts over 8 weeks)
No penalty for missing a week — your average accuracy still counts for the leaderboard weight
The consensus
Each week your prediction contributes to a group consensus shown on the Predict tab. Your contribution is weighted by your accuracy over recent weeks — the better your track record, the more your call influences the group signal. New players start at neutral weight.
