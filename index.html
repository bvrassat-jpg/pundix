How it works
Weekly cycle
Mon–Thu
Window open · Early predictions earn a score bonus ⚡
Friday
Predictions lock at 23:59 UTC · Last chance, no bonus
Sat–Sun
BTC trades freely · Anticipation builds
Sunday
BTC weekly settlement fixed at 23:59 UTC · Rankings published
1
Submit one number
Enter the BTC/USD price you believe will be the weekly settlement. One number. Pure conviction. No confidence levels, no ranges — your exact call.
2
Meet your opponent
The moment you submit, Satoshi reveals his call for the week — his price, his reasoning, and your running record against him. No waiting for Sunday. You know straight away who's more bullish or bearish, and by how much.
3
Predict early, earn more
Predict on Monday and a correct call scores 1.5× your base score. Tuesday 1.3×, Wednesday 1.2×, Thursday 1.1×. The multiplier only applies if you're right — it never punishes a wrong call. Friday is the deadline with no bonus.
4
Share your prediction
A shareable card is generated instantly. Post it on X before the week plays out. Public commitment is the game.
5
Watch the live week
Your live position is on the Live Race — distance from BTC, who's ahead, who's exposed. No final scores until settlement. Just live distances and tension.
6
Compete on accuracy
Sunday settlement is the truth. Smallest error wins — multipliers applied where earned. Rankings are public. Share your result card — beat the crowd or take your loss publicly.
7
Build your track record
Season standings accumulate week by week. Consistency is rewarded. One prediction per week. Every week matters.

Satoshi Calibration
Stated confidence vs. actual accuracy, week by week. "Hit" = error ≤2% (your own "Very close" bar).
Regime Performance
Satoshi's average error, split by how volatile that week's actual BTC move was. Calm <3% · Normal 3–6% · Volatile >6% weekly move — bands chosen against the app's own "typical 2-8%" framing, not data-derived terciles, so they stay stable as more weeks resolve.
Direction & Bias
Does Satoshi call the right direction, and does the miss lean the same way every time? Bias % > 0 = calls too high; < 0 = calls too low.
Signal Cross-Correlation
Pairwise correlation across all signal readings on record. High |r| flags possible duplicate votes — this is exactly the pattern that caught On-chain and Social Lag secretly mirroring F&G.
