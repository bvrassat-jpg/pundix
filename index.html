The game
01
Predict
Every Monday, submit your price targets for EUR/USD and USD/CHF by Wednesday midnight. Just a number — where do you think the pair closes on Friday?
02
Score
Friday close prices determine your score. Accuracy is measured by how close your % move call is to the actual move. The closer your call, the higher your score. Consistent accuracy builds your rank.
03
Win
Weekly winner gets a bottle of Krug. Season champion wins a weekend for two in Verbier. Everyone is ranked publicly — your reputation is on the line.
Scoring formula
Score per pair = 100 − (|your % call − actual % move| × 100)
i.e. each 0.01% off costs 1 point · max 100 pts per pair per week · weekly total = 200 pts max
Season score = sum of all weekly scores (max 1600 pts over 8 weeks)
No penalty for missing a week — your average accuracy still counts for the leaderboard weight
The consensus
Each week your prediction contributes to a group consensus shown on the Predict tab. Your contribution is weighted by your accuracy over recent weeks — the better your track record, the more your call influences the group signal. New players start at neutral weight.
Prize Rules & Terms
Season prize
Weekend for two · Verbier
Awarded to the player with the highest cumulative score at the end of Season 1 (8 weeks). Dates to be agreed with the winner; valid until 31 December 2026.
Weekly prize
Bottle of Krug Grande Cuvée
Awarded each Friday to the player whose combined prediction for EUR/USD and USD/CHF is closest to the actual closing prices. One weekly prize per person per week.
· Open to anyone. Free to enter. No purchase necessary.
· Predictions must be submitted by Wednesday 23:59 CET each week to be eligible.
· Scoring is based on % accuracy vs ECB reference rate at Friday 16:00 CET close.
· In the event of a tie, the earliest submission wins.
· Players may submit one prediction per week. Multiple submissions void that week's entry.
· Prizes are non-transferable and have no cash equivalent.
· Pundix reserves the right to disqualify entries submitted in bad faith or by automated means.
· Season 1 runs for 8 consecutive weeks from the first prediction Monday.
· These rules may be updated before Season 1 begins. Participants will be notified by email.
